Accuracy is estimated from how confident the model was about each word it heard, so it works for anything you say. Efficiency compares processing time with the length of your speech: "5× faster" means 60 seconds of speech took about 12 seconds.

Built with OpenAI Whisper (base) and Transformers.js. Runs in your browser, so your voice never leaves your device.
